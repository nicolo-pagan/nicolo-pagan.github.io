About

I am an AI Research Scientist with a decade of experience in AI research and three years in production software engineering. My background is mathematical (game theory, dynamical systems and multi-agent strategic interaction), and I apply it to auditing economically consequential LLM decisions, from labor markets to social-media recommender systems.

I developed inverse-optimization frameworks for inferring agents' preferences, established the first formal taxonomy of feedback loops and biases in ML systems, and designed scalable LLM evaluation and auditing frameworks, fine-tuning workflows and RAG architectures across models and platforms. With Giotto.AI, I won a gold medal in the 2024 Kaggle ARC Challenge, one of AI's hardest benchmarks for abstract reasoning and few-shot generalization.

Algorithmic fidelity

Can LLMs stand in for people? Benchmarks that tell human from AI-generated social-media content at scale.

Responsible AI & fairness
